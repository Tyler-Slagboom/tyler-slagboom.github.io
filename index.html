Cats!

A topic that I find interesting are cats!
I've liked cats ever since I was a child, my family getting our first and only one when
I was only five years old. At the time, she was older than me being seven years old, and
we had her all the way until I was 16 years old, when she sadly passed away at the age of 18!
Today, I have a cat all of my own named Mako, who I obtained from a colleague of a friend.
My roomate and I actually thought that he was a girl when we first got him, naming him
Maple. Here's a link to the Sacramento SPCA if you're interesting in getting
a kitten of your own.
